# Zeitguru during the ExampleCompany evaluation

This temporary feature is for `https://office.example.test` and its verified Zeitguru tenant only. `ZEITGURU_EVALUATION_ENABLED` defaults to false. Enabling it with another Core origin, without Core authentication, or without mandatory encryption fails startup. Reconsider whether the feature belongs in any future deployment; do not copy the company flag or credentials to the public review environment.

## Employee setup

Open Time and expand **Zeitguru · ExampleCompany evaluation** below the clock. Sign in with your own Zeitguru account using the same company email as DCO. Confirm the returned identity, select an assigned clocking location, and consent to forwarding work start, break start, break end and work end. You can connect while working or taking a break in Zeitguru. DCO adopts the recorded start and breaks without sending a new clock-in. A separate or corrected overlapping DCO shift must be reviewed first.

Use DCO's normal clock buttons while connected. The clock checks Zeitguru every 30 seconds while visible, when returning to the tab, and before a new DCO punch. You can also choose **Sync with Zeitguru now**. Recorded external starts, breaks and stops update the linked DCO shift with an audit trail; completed recent external shifts are included from connection consent onward. Approved DCO corrections and unrelated overlapping entries are preserved for review. Older history remains an explicit import. Avoid clicking both systems at exactly the same time because the provider has no conditional write API. Imports, leave, requests, manual corrections, work-location-only changes and automatic clock-outs do not forward. A location selected for the connection is a Zeitguru branch, distinct from DCO's office/home-office label.

**Saved in DCO** and **confirmed in Zeitguru** are separate states. Pending or uncertain delivery is visible beside the clock. Recorded DCO actions remain saved during provider problems. A new action waits for a successful synchronization check so it cannot overwrite a newer provider clock state. Check the affected punch in Zeitguru; “Check delivery” only reads and never resends. If automatic confirmation is impossible, check all pending punches there, finish any running shift in both systems, and explicitly confirm the manual review. This clears the delivery queue as reviewed without asserting that it was delivered or changing the provider's records.

Disconnect removes the stored authentication material, cancels known unsent work, and preserves uncertain history for review. The same provider account can be reauthenticated for reconciliation. Another DCO employee cannot take over its unresolved connection.

## Import your previous Zeitguru history

Under the same Zeitguru panel, open **View and import history** and choose **Import my Zeitguru history**. A connected account can import directly. Otherwise verify your own Zeitguru login first; that short-lived verification can be used just for the import, including during an active shift, without activating clock forwarding. A history-only verification is consumed after the attempt and is not saved as a forwarding connection.

Zeitguru generates its CSV in the browser. This button reads the same employee clocking records and their associated credit data directly, so no manual CSV download or upload is needed. It reads only the verified employee's history, in monthly ranges, and includes completed shifts whose start and end are before today in Europe/Berlin. Current and unfinished shifts are omitted. Reading failure or the 10,000-record / 240-month safety bound aborts before any archive commit; malformed or ambiguous individual rows are counted and reported as skipped.

The imported archive contains dates, original start/end times, breaks, recorded work, approval status, and identifiable Zeitguru work credits. Paid breaks, automatic closures, overnight shifts and daylight-saving differences preserve the historical source behavior. Missing or ambiguous credit is displayed as unknown and excluded from the credited total; extra credit components and source discrepancies are reported. Raw source evidence is retained encrypted for reconciliation and is never sent to the browser.

The import also reads the verified employee's dated contracts, approved absences, configured holidays, opening credit and manual credit corrections. It commits this encrypted accounting evidence together with the clock archive. Historical overtime, day checks, calendars and exports use the resulting daily ledger. Physical recorded work and credited time remain separate; paid absences, paid breaks and corrections are not reconstructed from timestamps. The latest active full snapshot supplies the accounting view, avoiding repeated-import double counting.

If Zeitguru has no opening credit, DCO displays period changes and marks the earlier balance as unconfirmed. It does not infer employment start from a default contract date, invent earlier carry or combine unverified prior-year changes into a settled current balance. Missing source holiday years and conflicting or unfinished records remain visible for review. Vacation allowance is not inferred from approved leave. Source schedules provide expected work for up to 400 days beyond the imported period; a later explicit DCO employment term takes precedence. Reimport after source changes or before the forecast coverage expires.

Native DCO punches, employee settings and the Zeitguru connection remain independent. Imported intervals are read-only; approved native corrections take precedence for that date and retain a review marker. The existing administrative CSV importer remains a separate workflow.

**Days needing attention** counts distinct employee days with review findings. Ordinary schedule differences and eight-hour advisories remain available in the separate **Information** section and do not, on their own, ask for a correction. Missing records, source discrepancies, invalid intervals and other concrete checks remain in the attention list. Full approved days off, including time off in lieu with no credit, are not reported as missing attendance. An active timer today is not treated as an unfinished historical day. Every imported daily ledger remains available under **View and import history → Review daily values**, including days with no findings.

An unchanged source record is not imported twice. Changed source records or overlapping intervals are reported as conflicts and existing archive records remain unchanged; source ledger credits remain available with review warnings. **Undo this import** removes its accounting overlay and unchanged records inserted by that batch. Undo newer dependent imports first. An import containing only duplicates can still be undone when it added an accounting snapshot. Undo restores the preceding active snapshot, or native DCO accounting when none remains; it does not erase native entries, other employees, the connection or anything in Zeitguru. Import and undo keep an encrypted batch audit. Backups may retain earlier snapshots according to company retention. Import request IDs make a retry after a lost response return its existing receipt instead of repeating the operation.

## Data and delivery behavior

The password is used once for sign-in, then discarded. The server stores the resulting provider session encrypted with the Time data-encryption key and rotates it on refresh. A short-lived verification challenge expires after three minutes. Tokens, private provider configuration and delivery payloads are excluded from browser state, logs and audit details. Connection data and the journal reside in the Time SQLite database and are included in its encrypted backups.

Each accepted normal DCO action and its delivery intent commit in one SQLite transaction. A client operation ID and clock revision reject duplicates and competing stale requests. Before network delivery, the complete provider mutation is persisted encrypted. Delivery is ordered per employee. Confirmation requires reading back both the expected clock row and credited-time value. A timeout, crash, expired lease, provider mismatch or unknown result stops that employee's queue; no blind retry occurs. Ordinary new punches expire after two minutes. Recovery can merge captured actions up to 24 hours old only when their journal has no saved delivery plan, the oldest failure was a pre-write conflict, provider history retains the confirmed prefix, and all captured actions follow its latest minute. Original intent IDs and timestamps remain unchanged. The merged local shift and complete delivery plans commit atomically. Any saved plan or uncertain write requires read-back evidence and is never blindly retried; a later provider action can confirm a retained earlier punch only with consistent credit. Restored queues without this specific conflict evidence remain for review.

The adapter uses the observed Zeitguru browser protocol: an atomic Firebase update containing the clock row and its associated credit leaf. Recorded-break precedence, minute precision, overnight dates, unique credit prefixes and approval settings are checked. Unsupported personalized/capping/paid-break rules and ambiguous daylight-saving transitions fail before writing. This is an experimental adapter to the vendor's web client, not a verified supported public integration API. Firebase's multipath update has no conditional ETag operation; another external edit can occur between preflight and delivery. Fresh checks detect many conflicts but cannot guarantee remote transaction isolation or exactly-once behavior across other clients. Provider changes can require an adapter update.

## Acceptance and operation

Before depending on forwarding, one consenting employee must perform a real work start, break start/end and work end in DCO, checking each provider confirmation and the final Zeitguru credited minutes. No artificial employee punches should be created for this test. Then repeat with a second employee's independently connected account. Verify authentication, own-account reads, server time and mutation planning in your authorized evaluation environment before enabling forwarding.

Resolve any unsupported settings shown during connection or delivery before extending the evaluation. A provider-supported API and contractual confirmation remain desirable before relying on this beyond the temporary evaluation. An administrator can stop forwarding by disabling the company feature and restarting Time; preserve the database/journal for review. Disabling does not close a shift already open in Zeitguru.

Backups contain encrypted provider sessions. Keep the encryption key separate. Restored pending operations must be reviewed, never bulk-resubmitted. Monitor the employee-visible attention states during the pilot; no external alert delivery has been configured.
