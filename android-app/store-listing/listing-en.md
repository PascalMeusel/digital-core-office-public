# Digital Core Office

## Short description (max 80 characters)

Time, inventory and projects on your own organization’s server.

## Full description

Digital Core Office brings time tracking, inventory management and project work
together in one app. Connect once to your organization’s Digital Core Office
server, then move seamlessly between the areas enabled by your administrator.

Features:

- Track working time and review questionable days with actionable suggestions
- Manage incoming goods, stock, locations, items and accountable change history
- Create QR labels and open them with a system-provided QR scanner
- Organize projects, tasks and responsibilities
- Use one account across all enabled systems
- Change server later and securely clear local session data

Digital Core Office is a client for self-hosted installations. A compatible server
operated by you or your organization is required. The publisher does not provide a
hosting service. The app is free, has no ads and contains no in-app purchases. A
separate server-software licence may be required depending on the number of users.

Audience: adult workplace users.
